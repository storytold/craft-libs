# craft-draw

Vector and shape tools for Craft apps. With the `ui` feature, `craft_draw::ui` has the pen, crop and free transform overlays (below), the gradient tools (`GradientEditor`, `GradientPalette`, `GradientSwatch`, `GradientRamp`, the stop rules in `gradient::stops`, the on-canvas `GradientOverlay` with its `gradient_line` geometry, and the `GradientMapEditor`) and these tools and panels, each in its own module with its labels:

- `stroke`: `StrokeEditor`, `StrokeButton` and `StrokePreview` over `StrokeStyle`; `corner`: `CornerWidgets` for corner radii;
- `shape` and `shape_drag`: `ShapePanel`, `ShapeBar`, `ShapeDragTool` and `CreateShapeDialog`; `slice`: `SliceTool`, `SliceOverlay`, `SliceBar` and the Slice Options and Divide Slices dialogs; `artboard` and `add_beside`: `ArtboardTool`, `ArtboardBar`, `ArtboardProperties` and the add-beside buttons; `connector`: `ConnectorTool`, `ConnectorOverlay` and `ConnectorBar`;
- `pattern` and `picture_fill`: `PatternFillEditor`, `PatternFillDialog` and `PictureFillEditor`;
- `transform`: `TransformSession` with `TransformOverlay`, `TransformBar` and the Transform menu words; `crop`: `CropTool`, `CropSession` and `CropBar` beside the `CropFrame`; `measure`: `RulerTool`, `RulerBar` and `box_distances`; `symmetry`: `Symmetry` with `SymmetryOverlay`, `SymmetryMenu` and `SymmetryTransform`;
- `brush_presets` and `brush_settings`: `BrushPresetPicker`, `BrushPresetChip` and `BrushSettingsPanel` (its answer is `BrushSettingsPanelOutput`).

The main types of every module are re-exported at `craft_draw::ui`.

The gradient editor uses the colour picker and wells of `craft-color`.

`ui::pen`, `ui::crop` and `ui::transform` hold the canvas overlays of the Pen, Crop and Free Transform tools. They sense through `craft_ui::canvas` (`CanvasView`, `Claim`, `PartFocus`, `quad`):

`TransformBox` draws Free Transform's box over the guides (a bare 1 pt line, 8 square handles, the reference point) and runs its gestures through `quad::drag` (PhotoCraft's rules by default; `BoxRules`, `RotateZone` and `inside_moves` set DesignCraft's, VectorCraft's or a Move tool's): the nearest handle within 12 pt, drags reported as edit gestures, a click outside or a double-click inside or Enter applies, Esc undoes a drag and then discards the box, the arrows move the picked part, an optional readout shows the angle, scale or move; one Tab stop whose Page keys pick the whole box, a corner, an edge or the reference point, each an AccessKit object named by the host's `TransformNames`. A host that runs its own input calls `hit`, `press`, `track` and `release` (canvas points, modifiers, the view; `BoxPress` is the state it keeps between press and release) and gets the box `show` would give; `paint_pivot` draws the reference point for a host that shows one outside the box. `BezierOverlay` draws paths (`EditPath`: subpaths of `Knot`s, each an anchor with in and out handles; `PathSelection` is the host's) with their anchors and direction handles and edits them as Direct Selection (select, ⇧ toggle, ⌥ subpath, drag anchors, handles and segments, a marquee on empty canvas) or Convert Point does, reporting `PathEdit`s for the host to apply; one Tab stop whose Page keys pick anchors and their handles, arrows move them, Insert and Delete report anchors to add and remove; above `MAX_ANCHORS` anchors it draws outlines only. `PenDraft` draws the path being drawn with the Pen and runs its gestures (click for a corner anchor, drag for symmetric handles, ⇧ at 45°, ⌥ breaks the pair, a press on the last anchor retracts its handle, a press on the first closes the path, a press on an open path's end resumes it) and keys (Enter and Esc end the path and keep it, Backspace and `PenDraft::remove_last` take back the last anchor), reporting the whole draft as an `EditSubpath` edit gesture; it is not a Tab stop and its anchors are AccessKit objects named by the host's `PenNames`.
